Privacy Policy

Your genetic data privacy is our top priority. Learn how we protect your information.

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Genova AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and genetic information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our genetic analysis platform.

By using Genova AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information
  • Account information (username, email address)
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Communication preferences
2. Genetic Data
  • SNP (Single Nucleotide Polymorphism) data from uploaded files
  • Analysis results including ancestry predictions and trait analyses
  • Sample identifiers and metadata
3. Usage Information
  • Log data and analytics
  • Device and browser information
  • Feature usage patterns

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing Services: To perform genetic analysis and generate predictions
  • Improving Accuracy: To enhance our AI models and prediction algorithms
  • Communication: To send you analysis results and important updates
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and ensure data integrity
  • Research: Anonymized, aggregated data may be used for scientific research (with consent)

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

End-to-end encryption for data transmission
Encrypted storage for genetic data
Regular security audits
Access controls and authentication

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data and account
  • Portability: Export your data in a standard format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of data processing at any time

Data Retention

We retain your genetic data and analysis results for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion of your data at any time. Upon account deletion, your personal and genetic data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
