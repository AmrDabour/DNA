Frequently Asked Questions
What file formats do you support?

We support CSV files containing SNP data with columns for rsID, chromosome, position, and genotype.

Is my genetic data secure?

Yes! We use encryption for data transmission and storage. Your data is never shared without consent.

How accurate are the predictions?

Our ancestry predictions achieve ~94% accuracy and gender predictions ~98% accuracy on HapMap data.

Can I delete my data?

Absolutely. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your profile settings.

Is this open source?

Check out our GitHub repository for more information about the project and how to contribute.
